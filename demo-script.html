Act 2
AI Daily Briefing — "InGen reads everything so you don't have to"
⏱️ 0:35 – 1:30 (55 seconds)
🖱️ Dashboard is loaded. The AI Daily Briefing section is prominent at the top. Point out the stat bar first.

The moment you land on InGen, three numbers tell you your day: emails received, urgent items, and meetings.

🖱️ Hover over each stat card: "Today's Emails", "Urgent", "Meetings Today"

But the real power is up here.

🖱️ Gesture toward the AI Daily Briefing card with the ✨ sparkle icon.

This is your AI Daily Briefing. Every morning, InGen's local AI engine — running entirely on your MacBook — analyzes your emails, your calendar, and even the Quip documents linked inside those emails.

⏸️ Let them read the briefing text for 3 seconds

It pulls out what matters. The escalation that needs your attention by noon. The doc review your PM is waiting on. The 1:1 where your direct report flagged a blocker last Thursday.

🖱️ Scroll down slightly to show "Top Priorities" with urgency badges — High, Medium.

These aren't generic summaries. These are prioritized, context-aware action items — generated from YOUR data. And notice the linked documents section — InGen automatically fetched and summarized Quip docs that were referenced in your emails.

🖱️ Point to the "📄 Linked Documents" section if visible.

And here's what makes this different: this briefing streams in word by word — like ChatGPT — the first time. After that, it's cached. Instant. Under three seconds.

💡 Emphasize "entirely on your MacBook" and "zero cloud" — this is the differentiator
Act 3
Email Triage + AI Draft — "Your inbox, pre-triaged by AI"
⏱️ 1:30 – 2:15 (45 seconds)
🖱️ The "Email Triage" tab should already be active. Show the priority swim lanes.

Now let's look at email. InGen doesn't just show you a list — it triages for you.

🖱️ Point to the three swim lanes: 🔴 Respond Now, 🟡 Respond Today, 🟢 FYI

Three lanes. Red — respond now, these are urgent. Yellow — respond today. And green — FYI, collapsed by default, because you don't need the noise.

🖱️ Click the date range filter — switch from "Today" to "7 days" to show more emails loading in.

You can filter by time range — today, seven days, fourteen, thirty — and it paginates automatically.

🖱️ Click on a red/urgent email card to expand it. Show the email body.

Let me expand this one. Now watch this —

🖱️ Click the "✨ Draft Reply" button on the email card. Wait for the AI draft to generate.

One click. InGen generates a draft reply using Retrieval Augmented Generation. It searched through two hundred of my past sent emails to match my writing style, pulled context from the thread, and wrote a response I can send — or tweak — in seconds.

⏸️ Let the draft text display for 3 seconds

That's not a template. That's AI that knows how I write.

Act 4
Dive Deep Chat — "Ask your data anything"
⏱️ 2:15 – 2:45 (30 seconds)
🖱️ Scroll down to the AI Chat interface at the bottom of the dashboard. Click into the chat input field.

But what if you don't want a summary — you want a specific answer?

🖱️ Type: "What did I discuss with the team about the launch timeline last week?" and press Enter.

InGen has a conversational AI chat — backed by vector search over all your emails and calendar data. Ask it anything.

⏸️ Wait for the response to stream in — it should show cited sources

Look at that — it found the relevant email threads, cross-referenced my calendar, and gave me a sourced answer. This is like having a personal Kendra — except it's private, local, and it's searching YOUR data, not a shared index.

Act 5
Week Ahead — "Know your week before it starts"
⏱️ 2:45 – 3:15 (30 seconds)
🖱️ Click "Week Ahead" in the sidebar navigation.

Let's zoom out from today to the whole week.

🖱️ The Week Ahead page loads showing a 7-day calendar view with day cards.

Week Ahead gives you a day-by-day breakdown of your next seven days. Each day shows your meeting count, your meeting load level — light, balanced, moderate, heavy — and it automatically identifies deep work slots.

🖱️ Point to a day card that shows "Deep Work" slots or 1:1 detection badges.

See this? InGen found a ninety-minute gap on Wednesday — that's your protected deep work window. And it flagged your 1:1s so you can prep for those specifically.

🖱️ Scroll down to the AI Weekly Coaching Brief section.

And down here — the AI Weekly Coach. This is a personalized coaching brief generated by AI that references your actual meeting names. It covers preparation priorities, energy pacing across heavy and light days, strategic opportunities, and scheduling risk alerts.

This is the brief I wish someone handed me every Sunday night.

Act 6
Leadership Analytics — "The dashboard you wish you had at your first staff meeting"
⏱️ 3:15 – 3:50 (35 seconds)
🖱️ Click "Leadership" in the sidebar.

Now — Leadership Analytics. This is where InGen becomes an operating system for managers.

🖱️ The Leadership page loads. Show the Time Audit section first.

Time Audit — how many hours in meetings versus deep work? What's the breakdown by meeting type — 1:1s, small groups, large meetings, all-hands?

🖱️ Point to the meeting type breakdown and the balance assessment.

InGen gives you the balance assessment with full transparency. You can toggle between seven, fourteen, and thirty-day views.

🖱️ Scroll to Relationship Health section.

Relationship Health — every key contact scored from zero to one hundred based on communication patterns. Green means healthy. Red means neglected. It pulls your direct reports from Phonetool automatically.

🖱️ Point to a relationship card that shows "At Risk" or "Neglected" status.

This one's flagged "At Risk" — I haven't had meaningful communication in twelve days. That's the kind of signal that prevents surprises in Forte.

🖱️ Quickly scroll past Action Items and Blockers sections.

Below that — AI-extracted action items organized by ownership, and auto-detected blockers from your email and meeting content.

Act 7
Team Health & Engineering Metrics — "Operational excellence, automated"
⏱️ 3:50 – 4:35 (45 seconds)
🖱️ Click "Team Health" in the sidebar.

Team Health — your WBR goals, always up to date.

🖱️ The My Team / WBR page loads showing the goal board with status color cards.

InGen pulls goals directly from Taskei, parses the status, and gives you this at-a-glance board. Green, yellow, red — with an AI-generated health summary at the top.

🖱️ Point to the summary cards: On Track, Needs Attention, Completed, Stale.

But here's the powerful part —

🖱️ Point to or click on the ECD tracking section — missed ECDs, upcoming ECDs.

ECD tracking. InGen snapshots your estimated completion dates weekly and detects drift. It knows when a date slipped, by how many days, and flags upcoming ECDs within a three-day window. No more spreadsheet archaeology.

🖱️ Click to expand a goal card to show child tasks if available.

And you can drill into any goal to see subtasks — recursive expansion, three levels deep.

🖱️ Click "Code Metrics" in the sidebar.

Code Metrics — this is the engineering velocity dashboard.

🖱️ The Eng Metrics page loads showing the org summary and engineer table.

Per-engineer code review activity pulled from code.amazon.com. CRs created, CRs reviewed, review ratios. These mini sparklines show eight-week trends at a glance.

🖱️ Point to the sparkline column and the week-over-week delta arrows.

Week-over-week deltas. And if an engineer has three consecutive weeks of declining output — the row highlights amber. Early signal, not a surprise.

🖱️ Click "Ticket Health" in the sidebar.

And Ticket Health — your resolver groups at a glance. Open tickets, aging breakdown, SLA status. The operational hygiene dashboard every SDM needs.

🖱️ Show the summary cards and resolver group table briefly — don't linger.
